Travel to The Bahamas

Welcome to Paradise!

The Bahamas is a beautiful place to visit. It has many beaches, resorts, and activities to do. You can go swim with the pigs, scuba diving, or just relax on the beach. The Bahamas is a great place for a vacation!

Every tenth of july, the Bahamas has a independence day parade called the Junkanoo. Its filled with color and music and celebration!
